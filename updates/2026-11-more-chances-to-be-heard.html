More Chances to Be Heard

The first round of Your Library, Your Voice listening sessions has wrapped, and the conversations were everything we hoped for. So we're adding nine more — including late-evening virtual sessions you can join from home once the kids are in bed.

Over the past few weeks, residents came together in the library to talk about what they love about the Main Library and the Bellevue Avenue Branch and what they hope these buildings can become. The turnout was wonderful and the conversations were honest, generous, and full of ideas. Every one of them is going straight into the design and community-engagement process with DIG Architecture.

What we heard

Across twelve sessions, a clear set of priorities surfaced again and again. The word cloud below is a snapshot: the larger the words, the more often residents raised them.

Why we're adding more

Two things became clear along the way. First, the appetite to be part of this is bigger than a single round of sessions. And second, "normal" hours simply don't work for everyone. We received messages that between work and young children, the only free moment in the day comes after bedtime. So we listened, and we built this next round around that.

Alongside more daytime and in-person sessions, we've added late-evening virtual sessions at 9:00 PM that you can join from your couch after the house goes quiet. No babysitter, no commute — just you, your ideas, and a screen.
